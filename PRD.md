# Product Requirements Document

## Project Overview

This project is a professional portfolio website for Ariel. The main purpose of the website is to support her job search and give recruiters a better understanding of her background beyond what they can see on a traditional resume.

Ariel has a unique combination of experiences in interior design, business, finance, athletics, and different cultural environments. The website should bring these parts of her background together in one clear and professional place.

The site should work for recruiters in both interior design and finance/business-related fields without making Ariel look limited to only one career path.

## Target Audience

The primary audience is:

* Recruiters
* Hiring managers
* Potential employers
* Professional connections

Visitors should be able to understand Ariel's background quickly and easily find more detailed information if they are interested.

## Main Goals

The website should:

1. Introduce Ariel and her background.
2. Show her education and professional experience.
3. Highlight her experience as a four-year varsity student-athlete.
4. Show the connection between her interior design, business, and finance background.
5. Highlight her multicultural experience.
6. Give recruiters easy ways to contact her, view her LinkedIn, and download her resume.

## Personal Brand

The website should communicate three main parts of Ariel's background.

### Student-Athlete

Ariel trained seriously in golf in Florida and later received a full scholarship to attend SCAD through golf. She competed as a varsity student-athlete for four years.

This experience should help communicate qualities such as discipline, commitment, time management, and the ability to balance academics with athletics.

Golf does not need its own page. It should be included naturally as part of her story on the Home and About pages.

### Cross-Disciplinary Background

Ariel's undergraduate major at SCAD was Interior Design, with a minor in Business.

She is currently a graduate student at Columbia University's SIPA, where her studies are focused on finance.

The website should show this transition clearly. Her design background and current finance education should feel connected rather than presented as two unrelated areas.

### Multicultural Background

Ariel was born in Beijing and later attended high school in Florida. She has studied and lived in different cultural environments in China and the United States.

She speaks Chinese and English and has beginner-level Spanish.

Her multicultural background should be part of her personal story, but the website should communicate it naturally rather than making it feel like a separate list of facts.

## Site Structure

The website will have five main pages:

### Home

The Home page should give recruiters a quick introduction to Ariel.

It should include:

* Ariel's name
* Professional headshot
* Short introduction
* Current Columbia graduate student status
* Interior Design + Business + Finance background
* Multicultural background
* Four-year varsity student-athlete experience
* Main navigation
* Contact button
* LinkedIn button
* Resume button

The first screen should communicate what makes Ariel different without requiring the visitor to read a large amount of text.

### About

The About page should tell Ariel's story in a more personal way.

It should include:

* Beijing background
* Transition to studying in the United States
* Golf training in Florida
* Student-athlete experience
* SCAD experience
* Transition from interior design and business into finance
* Current graduate studies at Columbia
* Languages

The writing should remain professional but should feel more personal than a resume.

### Education

This page should show Ariel's educational timeline clearly.

Each school should include the institution, degree or field of study, graduation year, and relevant information.

SCAD should include her Interior Design major, Business minor, and student-athlete experience.

Columbia SIPA should show her current graduate education and finance focus.

### Experience

The Experience page should include both professional experience and selected interior design projects.

#### Professional Experience

Each experience should include:

* Organization/company
* Position
* Dates
* Location if relevant
* Short description or selected responsibilities/accomplishments

All information should come directly from Ariel's resume.

#### Selected Interior Design Projects

Relevant SCAD or personal design projects can also appear on this page.

Projects may include:

* Project name
* Short description
* Renderings or project images
* Design concept
* Relevant tools or skills

Only available and approved project materials should be included.

### Contact

The Contact page should make it easy for recruiters to connect with Ariel.

It should include:

* Email
* LinkedIn
* Resume download

Ariel's preferred professional email should be confirmed before the site is finalized.

## Calls to Action

The three main actions throughout the website are:

* Email Ariel
* View LinkedIn
* Download Resume

These should be easy to find, especially on the Home and Contact pages.
