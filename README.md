Ariel's Portfolio Website

About the Project

For this project, I am designing and building a professional portfolio website for my older sister, Ariel. She is currently preparing for her job search, and the goal of this website is to give recruiters a more complete view of her background than a traditional resume can provide.

Ariel was born in Beijing and later moved to the United States, where she completed high school in Florida. She continued her professional golf training in Florida and earned a full scholarship to attend the Savannah College of Art and Design (SCAD). At SCAD, she studied Interior Design with a minor in Business while competing as a varsity student-athlete for four years. After graduating in 2025, she continued her education at Columbia University SIPA, where she is currently pursuing graduate studies with a focus on finance.

One of the main goals of this website is to show the different sides of Ariel's background without limiting her to one career path. Her undergraduate education gave her a foundation in design and business, while her current graduate studies are expanding her experience into finance. The website will include her education, professional experience, selected interior design projects, student-athlete experience, resume, and contact information so that it can support opportunities in both design and business-related fields.

I also want the website to reflect Ariel's international and multicultural experience. Growing up and studying in both Chinese and American environments has been an important part of her personal and academic journey. She speaks Chinese and English, with beginner-level Spanish, and has experience adapting to different academic, professional, and cultural settings.

Because Ariel is my sister, I have a good understanding of both her background and her personal preferences. This has helped me make design decisions that feel appropriate for her rather than relying entirely on a standard portfolio template. She prefers a clean and minimal style and likes light blue, so the website will use a light blue, white, and neutral color palette with a simple layout and clear navigation.

The final goal is to create a website that feels professional and easy for recruiters to navigate while still giving visitors a better sense of Ariel as a person, student, athlete, and developing professional.
