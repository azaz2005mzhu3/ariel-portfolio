# Client Brief

**Client:** Ariel, my older sister

**Purpose:**
Create a professional personal website to help Ariel with her job search. The website will introduce her background, education, experiences, and skills in a more personal way than a traditional resume.

**Audience:**
Recruiters, hiring managers, and potential employers.

**Key action:**
Visitors should be able to quickly understand Ariel's background, learn about her education and experience, and find a way to contact or connect with her.

**Pages needed:**

* Home
* About
* Education
* Experience
* Contact

**Content status:**
I already have most of Ariel's information, including her education background, work experience, resume, photos, and other information needed for the website. Since Ariel is my older sister, I also know her preferences and background well, which makes it easier for me to understand what she wants from the website.

**Important content:**
Ariel wants the website to include her full educational background and professional experience. She also wants to highlight her multicultural background because it is an important part of who she is. The website should show both her professional experience and her ability to understand and work with people from different cultures.

**Style preferences:**
Ariel prefers a simple and professional website. She likes blue, so I plan to use blue as the main accent color with neutral colors such as white, gray, and black. She does not want the website to look too complicated or have too many design elements. The overall style should be clean, modern, and easy to navigate.

**Inspiration sites:**
Simple personal portfolio and resume websites with a lot of white space, clear sections, minimal design, and blue accents. I want the website to feel professional but still personal to Ariel.
