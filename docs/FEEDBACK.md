# Client Feedback

## Second Client Conversation

**Date:** September 30, 2026  
**Client:** Ariel

After the Mini Project 1 showcase, I had a second conversation with my client, my sister Ariel. We looked through the first version of the website together, including viewing it on a phone, and talked about what she liked and what she wanted to improve.

## Client Feedback

Ariel said, "I think the website does a good job balancing the style I wanted. It is simple and clean, but it still looks professional."

She liked the overall direction of the website and felt that it matched the aesthetic she originally wanted.

She also wanted her PDF resume to be directly available on the website so recruiters could easily access it. For the Experience section, she wanted visitors to be able to learn more about the companies she previously worked for. She felt that if someone did not already know a company, having an easy way to learn more about it would give them a better understanding of her experience.

We also looked at the Contact section and decided that the email button should make contacting her as simple as possible.

## Changes Based on Feedback

After our conversation, I made three changes to the website:

- **Added Ariel's PDF resume.** Visitors can now access her full resume directly from the website.
- **Added official company website links.** The company names in the Experience section now link to their official websites so visitors can easily learn more about each company.
- **Updated the Send an Email button.** Clicking the button now automatically opens the visitor's email application with Ariel's email address ready to use.

These changes give recruiters more information and make it easier to contact Ariel while keeping the website simple and professional.
